# Model card – Expected Goals Baseline

## Intended use

Support exploratory shot-quality analysis, software demonstrations and analyst discussion.

## Not intended for

Contract decisions, player valuation, medical decisions, betting, public ranking or tactical decisions without validation.

## Training data

Synthetic event data generated by the repository. The distribution imitates plausible shot locations and context but is not real competition data.

## Features

Distance to goal, shot angle, pressure, first-time shot, assisted shot, body part and play pattern.

## Evaluation

Match-aware train/test split. Metrics include discrimination, probability accuracy and calibration.

## Limitations

- No goalkeeper position
- No defender locations or freeze-frame context
- No shot speed, technique or preceding-action sequence
- Synthetic outcomes can overstate apparent model stability
- No temporal, competition or provider transportability validation

## Production requirements

Use licensed data, version every dataset and feature definition, benchmark against a naive model, perform temporal validation, calibrate by competition, monitor drift and require analyst sign-off.
